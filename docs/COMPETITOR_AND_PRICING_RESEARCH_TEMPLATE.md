# Competitor and Pricing Research Template

**Status: template only.** Do not enter a competitor name, price, feature, privacy statement, ranking, or comparison conclusion without a current public source and date. Do not use this file to imply that EXE is cheaper, better, more private, or validated.

## Research rules

- Use publicly available, lawful sources. Record exact source URL, source/publication date where available, and date accessed.
- Quote or summarize only what the source supports. Mark unclear, unavailable, or region-dependent information as such.
- Keep screenshots/exports outside the repository unless they contain no personal data and the owner approves storage.
- Treat free-tier and paid prices as time-, currency-, location-, tax-, and plan-dependent until verified.
- Public claims require current, cited source evidence and owner review. One source does not prove a broad market conclusion.

## Do not claim lower price until comparison is complete

Do not make a “lower price,” “more affordable,” “better value,” “cheaper,” or equivalent claim until:

- the relevant alternatives and user-substitutes are defined from research;
- current plan/price, currency, region, tax, and access conditions are cited for each comparison;
- EXE’s own proposed pricing/cost assumptions are documented separately;
- target-user research supports which value and payment model matter;
- the owner/team reviews the evidence and wording.

Until then, describe pricing and positioning as open research questions only.
