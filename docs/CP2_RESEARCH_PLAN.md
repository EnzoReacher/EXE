# CP2 Research Plan — M8 Preparation

## Status and objective

**Status: prepared research plan; no research evidence has been collected in this file.**

M8 prepares the owner to validate EXE’s product value before selecting the next product feature, target segment, positioning, or price. The objective is to learn whether the proposed workflow reduces uncertainty and manual effort for job seekers working on one specific application.

CP2 remains **pending** until real, consent-safe evidence is collected, anonymized, entered in `CP2_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`, and reviewed by the owner/team.

## Target user

Job seekers actively applying for roles using an existing CV. Recruitment should seek people who have recently tailored, or expect soon to tailor, an application to a particular job description. This is a research definition, not a validated market segment.

## Hypotheses to test

### Primary product hypothesis

EXE can reduce uncertainty and manual effort when a job seeker tailors their application to a specific job.

### Value hypotheses

1. Users understand their match and gaps after seeing evidence-grounded findings.
2. Users trust evidence-grounded suggestions more than generic advice.
3. The roadmap and source-grounded draft save meaningful effort in a tailoring workflow.
4. Users consider the proposed value worth paying for.

These are hypotheses, not claims or completed findings.

## Research questions

1. What steps do participants currently take to tailor a CV to a job description?
2. Where do they spend time, lose confidence, or receive generic or conflicting advice?
3. Can they explain the meaning and limits of EXE’s supported, partly supported, unclear, and missing labels?
4. Does visible source evidence affect trust, understanding, or willingness to act?
5. Which parts of assessment, roadmap, draft, saved work, private review, and opportunity tracking are useful, confusing, or unnecessary?
6. What privacy concerns arise when a tool processes a CV and job description?
7. What alternatives do they currently use, and what evidence supports any comparison?
8. What value, if any, would justify payment? What free or paid expectations do they have?
9. Which user group and workflow should be prioritized next, if any?

## Signals to watch

| Area | Potential supporting signal | Warning signal |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding | Participant can accurately explain a label and locate the supporting excerpt without coaching. | Participant mistakes labels for a skill verdict, hiring score, or prediction. |
| Trust | Participant says the evidence/caveat makes the recommendation easier to inspect. | Participant cannot tell where a recommendation came from or doubts its boundary. |
| Effort | Participant describes a concrete step the roadmap/draft could shorten or organize. | Workflow adds steps, repeats existing work, or does not solve a meaningful problem. |
| Usability | Participant completes a fictional walkthrough with limited assistance and identifies clear next actions. | Repeated confusion, inaccessible controls, unclear privacy notices, or difficulty returning to saved work. |
| Privacy | Participant understands the local prototype boundary and can state a condition needed before sharing real CV data. | Participant expects guarantees not provided, or privacy concerns prevent intended use. |
| Value | Participant articulates a value trade-off in their own words. | Participant sees no distinct value or rejects the problem framing. |

Signals guide discussion; they are not score thresholds and do not turn a small sample into market validation.

## Minimum practical sample targets

- At least **5 target-user interviews**.
- At least **2 career/recruitment experts** (for example, career coaches, recruiters, hiring managers, or employability specialists).
- At least **20 anonymous survey responses**, where practical.

The course tracker may require a larger survey or other evidence. Confirm course requirements before declaring CP2 complete. Record recruitment constraints and sample limitations rather than silently treating a smaller sample as representative.

## Safe recruiting guidance

- Invite participants through lawful, appropriate channels without committing names or contact details to this repository.
- Explain that participation is voluntary and that they may skip questions or stop at any time.
- Ask participants **not** to share a full CV, personal contact information, account credentials, confidential employer information, live application material, or protected information.
- Use only the fictional EXE demo pack for walkthroughs unless a separate, owner-approved privacy process exists.
- Keep identity/contact details separate from anonymized notes. Commit only anonymous summaries if the owner decides repository storage is appropriate.
- Do not promise employment outcomes, payment, product access, or future features.

## Recommended research sequence

1. Complete M7 owner review using fictional data and record usability/privacy issues separately from CP2 findings.
2. Confirm the target-user definition, recruitment route, consent wording, note storage, and owner responsible for review.
3. Run 5+ semi-structured target-user interviews using `TARGET_USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`.
4. Run 2+ expert interviews using `EXPERT_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`.
5. Run the anonymous survey from `SURVEY_TEMPLATE.md` where practical.
6. Collect current, cited public alternative/pricing/privacy information using `COMPETITOR_AND_PRICING_RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md`; do not copy claims without source dates.
7. Enter anonymized evidence in `CP2_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`, label its strength and limitations, and have the owner/team review it.
8. Make a documented decision: retain, revise, narrow, or reject hypotheses; then select the next feature only from that decision.

## What M8 does not establish

This plan does not establish demand, usability, affordability, privacy compliance, willingness to pay, market size, competitor position, target segment, or a price. It is preparation for collecting evidence.
