# M8 Research and Owner-Review Package

## What is prepared

M8 prepares an evidence-collection and owner-review package without changing M0–M7 app behavior or collecting research automatically.

- [CP2 research plan](CP2_RESEARCH_PLAN.md)
- [Target-user interview guide](TARGET_USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md)
- [Expert interview guide](EXPERT_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md)
- [Anonymous survey template](SURVEY_TEMPLATE.md)
- [Blank evidence register](CP2_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md)
- [Competitor and pricing research template](COMPETITOR_AND_PRICING_RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md)
- [Owner execution checklist](M8_OWNER_EXECUTION_CHECKLIST.md)

## What the owner must do manually

1. Complete M7 browser, narrow-mobile, keyboard, review-link, saved-work, and opportunity-tracker checks using fictional data only.
2. Recruit participants ethically, obtain consent, and keep identity/contact information outside the repository.
3. Conduct interviews/survey and collect only consent-safe, anonymized notes.
4. Research alternatives, privacy, and pricing from current cited public sources.
5. Enter reviewed evidence and limitations in the register; then record a team/owner decision.

## What counts as genuine CP2 evidence

- Dated, consent-safe, anonymized notes from actual target-user and expert interviews.
- An aggregate, anonymous survey summary with collection method, response counts, limitations, and question-level results.
- Current cited public sources for market, alternatives, privacy, or pricing facts.
- A dated owner/team review that distinguishes observations from interpretations and decisions.

## What does not count as evidence

- This package, templates, fictional demos, code tests, owner assumptions, invented quotes/results, unsourced pricing, stale pages, testimonials without permission, or a small number of unreviewed comments.

## Decision after evidence is collected

After the owner/team reviews sufficient evidence, it may decide to retain, revise, narrow, or reject a product hypothesis; choose the next feature to investigate; identify a research-backed target segment; or keep pricing/positioning open. It may **not** claim EXE is lower priced, better value, validated, market-ready, or superior unless the recorded evidence directly supports the precise claim and the owner approves it.
