## Research design

Recruit approximately 10–15 final-year/recent IT graduates applying for Frontend Intern; observe 5–8 consented participants comparing prototype and their current workaround. Adjust numbers to recruitment and confirmed course rubric. Owner confirms deadline, CP1–CP4 requirements and what synthetic study input is acceptable. No recruitment messages are sent by this work.

## Interview script

1. Tell me about your most recent Frontend Intern application and project description task.
2. Which part was difficult? How often did this happen in the last month?
3. Show the steps you normally use with this fictional task, including any prompts or edits.
4. How do you decide which work was yours and which claim is safe to put on a CV?
5. What would make you distrust a suggested bullet? What would you remove?
6. After trying the prototype: which question was useful, redundant or confusing?
7. Would you return to this tool? Why? Ask willingness to pay neutrally, only after the task; no suggested price.

Avoid leading participants with 'Our tool is faster/more accurate'. Distinguish observed behavior from reported preference.

## Comparison protocol

Counterbalance task order across participants. Use equally difficult fictional projects/JDs; same time cap and instruction: produce up to three truthful English CV bullets. Baseline is participant's current ChatGPT prompt or CV builder; record the exact tool/version/date, relevant costs/access limits, and actual task interaction. Do not assert capabilities from memory. Official current source checks and actual workaround trials are OP-02 work, not completed research.

| Measure | Definition | Recording |
|---|---|---|
| Time | Start to approved output | Seconds; failed tasks remain failures |
| Edit burden | Wording edits and regeneration count | Counts + observed reasons |
| Claim accuracy | Supported atomic claims / unsupported claims | Blind review against supplied fact sheet |
| Relevance | Link to JD without adding facts | Consistent reviewer rubric, 1–5 |
| Completion | At least one accepted useful bullet | Yes/no + reason |
| Trust / reuse | Participant rating and explanation | 1–5 + anonymized quotation with consent |

## Short anonymous survey

Student stage; applying for Frontend Intern yes/no; project-CV-writing frequency; hardest step; current workaround; approximate time per project; experienced misleading suggestions yes/no/unsure; helpfulness after task 1–5; willingness to reuse yes/no/unsure; optional neutral price response; optional anonymous comment. No name, email, phone, repo account or full CV fields.
