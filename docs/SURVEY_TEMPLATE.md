# Anonymous Survey Template — CP2 Preparation

## Participant introduction and consent

> We are researching how job seekers tailor an existing CV to a specific job description. The survey is voluntary and anonymous. Do not provide your name, email, phone number, employer name, CV, job-application content, account details, or other personal/confidential information. You may skip questions or stop at any time. Responses will be summarized in aggregate and may be used to guide a student project; they do not guarantee a product, service, job outcome, or payment. By continuing, you agree to provide anonymous research feedback.
