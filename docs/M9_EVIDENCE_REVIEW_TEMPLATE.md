## Review rules

For every entry, keep these three levels separate:

1. **Observation** — what a participant actually said, did, or selected, or what a cited source actually states. Include evidence IDs/source links and do not add private details.
2. **Interpretation** — what the team thinks the observation may mean. State uncertainty, alternative explanations, and limitations.
3. **Decision** — what the team will retain, change, investigate, reject, or defer. Assign a decision owner and follow-up.

A small or convenient sample is not a general market claim. Do not replace a limitation with a conclusion.
