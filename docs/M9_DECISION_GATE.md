# M9 Product Decision Gate

## Purpose

M9 prevents a technically attractive feature, research template, or correctly formatted evidence register from becoming an unsupported product claim. It prepares — but does not make — the owner/team decision that allows M10 to select the next product feature.

## Required sequence

1. Complete the M7 owner review with fictional data, including desktop, narrow-mobile, keyboard, private review-link, and opportunity-tracker checks.
2. Have the owner/team/course review the M5 CP1 fictional demo and record the required evidence.
3. Collect CP2 research with the M8 guides; do not use committed real CVs or private application material.
4. Anonymize the evidence and enter only safe summaries in `CP2_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`.
5. Run `pnpm cp2:validate:collected`; correct structural issues or manually remove any sensitive content it reports.
6. Review evidence limitations, recruitment constraints, possible bias, and contradictions as an owner/team.
7. Classify each product hypothesis: **supported**, **partly supported**, **neutral**, **contradicted**, or **insufficient evidence**.
8. Decide whether to retain the current direction, narrow the target segment, revise the value proposition, remove a feature, improve usability, test pricing later, or collect more evidence.
9. Record the observation/interpretation/decision separation in `M9_EVIDENCE_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`.
10. Only after this decision may M10 select the next product feature with `M9_NEXT_FEATURE_SELECTION.md`.

## What this gate does not prove

- M9 does not complete CP2 automatically.
- Passing the validator does not prove product-market fit.
- A few interviews do not prove market demand.
- A survey does not automatically prove willingness to pay.
- A competitor source does not prove EXE is better, more private, easier to use, or superior.
- A lower price must be calculated from current, cited, comparable pricing data with region, currency, plan, tax, and access conditions considered.
- No claim may be published without owner/team review.
